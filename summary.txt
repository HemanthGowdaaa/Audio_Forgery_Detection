## 2. DETAIL EXPLANATION OF MODULES AND COMPONENTS

### A. Preprocessing Modules
*   **Audio Normalization & Loading (Traditional & Hybrid)**: 
    Audio is loaded as mono and resampled to 16,000 Hz. The signal is adjusted to a 
    fixed duration of 5.0 seconds. Clips longer than 5 seconds are center-cropped; 
    clips shorter are zero-padded. Amplitude normalization is applied, transforming 
    the raw signal to zero mean and unit variance.
*   **Spectrogram Generation (Hybrid)**: 
    Computes a Mel spectrogram with 128 frequency bands using a frame window size 
    (N_FFT) of 2048 and a step size (hop length) of 512 samples. The power spectrogram 
    is converted to decibel units (log-scale) to approximate human auditory perception. 
    It is then resized to 224x224 pixels and replicated to 3 channels to align with 
    standard deep learning backbones. Finally, ImageNet normalization is applied.
*   **Hand-crafted Feature Extraction (Traditional)**: 
    Extracts frame-by-frame values of:
    *   *MFCC (Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients)*: Represents the overall 
        spectral shape.
    *   *Chroma STFT*: Groups frequency spectrum energy into 12 semitone bins, 
        capturing harmonic qualities.
    *   *Spectral Contrast*: Computes the difference between peaks and valleys 
        in 7 octave-based sub-bands, exposing sub-band dynamics.
    *   *RMS (Root Mean Square)*: Computes envelope energy to track signal 
        amplitude variations.
    *   *ZCR (Zero Crossing Rate)*: Records the frequency of sign changes in 
        the signal, helping detect high-frequency noise and synthesis gaps.

### B. Core Classifier Architectures
*   **ResNet50 Backbone**:
    Processes 224x224x3 spectrograms, performing spatial downsampling and feature 
    extraction via residual blocks. The final fully connected classification layer is 
    removed, yielding a high-dimensional feature map of shape `[2048, 7, 7]`.
*   **CBAM (Convolutional Block Attention Module)**:
    Applies double attention:
    *   *Channel Attention*: Squeezes spatial dimension using both MaxPool and 
        AvgPool, passes results through a shared MLP, and sums them to scale 
        channel weights.
    *   *Spatial Attention*: Computes average and max values along the channel 
        dimension, concatenates them, and applies a 7x7 conv to direct spatial focus.
*   **Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Block**:
    Performs channel relationship scaling. Squeezes the channel map via global average 
    pooling into a 1D vector and passes it through an excitation bottleneck (reduction 
    ratio = 16) to scale channel maps by their relative importance.
*   **Transformer Branch**:
    Flattens spatial dimensions (`7x7 = 49` tokens) and adds learnable 1D positional 
    embeddings to inject temporal sequencing information. It then feeds tokens through 
    a Multi-Head Self-Attention (MHSA) encoder (8 heads, hidden dimension 4096), 
    enabling the model to associate acoustic features across distant frames of the audio.
*   **Multi-Scale Fusion (MSF) Block**:
    Uses four parallel conv branches with kernels 1x1, 3x3, 5x5, and 7x7 to extract 
    information at different resolutions. This allows the model to simultaneously 
    detect short-term tampering artifacts and long-term acoustic anomalies.
*   **Support Vector Machine (SVM)**:
    Utilizes aggregated statistical features (mean, std, min, max) of handcrafted 
    spectral representations. Standardizes features via z-score normalization and 
    classifies samples using a Radial Basis Function (RBF) kernel, which maps the 
    164-D feature space into higher dimensions for non-linear separation.

## 3. METHODOLOGY IMPLEMENTATION STEPS

1.  **Dataset Scanning and Validation**: 
    The preprocessing engine recursively scans `dataset/release_in_the_wild/`. 
    Audio files (`.wav`, `.mp3`, `.flac`) are checked for integrity. Missing or 
    damaged files are logged to `outputs/skipped_files.log`.
2.  **Stratified Data Splitting**: 
    The clean dataset is partitioned into 70% training, 15% validation, and 15% 
    testing splits. Class distributions (REAL vs. FAKE) are stratified across splits 
    to maintain equal class ratios. Manifests are saved as CSV files.
3.  **Feature Preprocessing Cache**: 
    Audio loading and feature extraction are computationally heavy. Feature caching 
    computes SHA-1 hash signatures from file paths and modification times. Feature 
    bundles are stored as `.npy` files to bypass feature recalculation on subsequent runs.
4.  **Traditional SVM Training Pipeline**:
    *   Selects a stratified subset of training samples (up to 6000) for tuning.
    *   Runs 3-fold Grid Search Cross-Validation (GridSearchCV) to locate optimal 
        values for C, gamma, and kernel hyper-parameters.
    *   Retrains on up to 12000 stratified samples using the selected parameters.
    *   Saves the resulting pipeline model to `outputs/svm_model.joblib`.
5.  **Hybrid ResNet++ Training Pipeline**:
    *   Initializes the hybrid deep neural network, loaded with ImageNet weights 
        for the ResNet50 backbone.
    *   Checks class balances and configures a Weighted Cross-Entropy Loss to counter 
        class distribution skew.
    *   Trains the model using AdamW optimizer with Cosine Annealing learning rate 
        scheduling (learning rate: 1e-4, weight decay: 1e-4).
    *   Optimizes using mixed precision (Automatic Mixed Precision - AMP) on CUDA 
        and clips gradients to 1.0.
    *   Monitors Validation F1 Score and triggers Early Stopping (patience: 10 epochs).
6.  **Unified Evaluation and Deployment**:
    *   Loads the best checkpoints from both classifiers and runs evaluation on the 
        identical, shared test split.
    *   Computes performance metrics: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, 
        ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Confusion Matrix, and ROC/PR curves.
    *   Compares F1 Scores, selects the best model, copies it to `best_model/` and 
        writes metadata summaries.
    *   Generates a standalone `report.html` interactive dashboard.

### C. Detailed Comparative Analysis

1.  **Sample Efficiency and Capped Subsets**:
    The most striking outcome of this project is the massive performance gap 
    favoring the traditional SVM model (98.60% accuracy vs. 62.50% accuracy). This 
    discrepancy is directly linked to the training configurations. 
    The training split was heavily capped in `config.yaml` (`train_subset: 160`). 
    While the SVM was trained on a larger subset (up to 6000 samples) and was able 
    to generalize using robust handcrafted features, the ResNet++ model was trained 
    on only 160 samples for 2 epochs. 
    Deep neural networks (especially complex hybrid models containing 155 million 
    parameters) are extremely data-hungry. Training such a network on 160 samples 
    for just 2 epochs does not give the network enough optimization steps to learn 
    meaningful spectral features. As a result, ResNet++ collapsed, predicting the 
    majority class (REAL) for all samples, yielding a F1-Score of 0.0%.

2.  **Handcrafted Features vs. Representation Learning**:
    Handcrafted features (MFCC, Chroma, Spectral Contrast, ZCR, and RMS) aggregate 
    domain-specific knowledge of audio signals. They capture spectral shape, harmonicity, 
    energy, and frequency crossovers directly. Because the feature dimensionality is 
    low (164 dimensions) and carries highly concentrated information, a simple SVM with 
    an RBF kernel can draw highly effective decision boundaries even with small amounts 
    of data. 
    On the other hand, ResNet++ must learn representations directly from 2D log-Mel 
    spectrograms. The raw input dimensionality is high (224 x 224 x 3 = 150,528 dimensions). 
    Learning attention maps (CBAM, SE), sequential dependencies (Transformer), and 
    multi-scale filters (MSF) from raw data requires thousands of training samples and 
    dozens of epochs to achieve convergence.

3.  **Computational Complexity and Training Time**:
    ResNet++ took 10,004.99 seconds (approx. 2.77 hours) to train for only 2 epochs 
    on the subset, whereas the SVM grid search and final training on 6000 samples 
    completed in just 284.82 seconds (approx. 4.7 minutes). Deep learning models present 
    heavy computational overhead, requiring accelerated hardware (GPU/MPS) and long 
    training runs.

4.  **Deployment Recommendations**:
    *   **Low-resource / Fast Deployment**: The SVM pipeline is the clear winner for 
        deploying immediately. It provides excellent accuracy (98.60%), high precision, 
        and low latency.
    *   **Production Deepfake Security**: While the current ResNet++ implementation 
        underperformed due to strict training limits, it remains the theoretically superior 
        architecture for detecting advanced AI voice clones and synthetic speech generator 
        artifacts that bypass handcrafted features. For production deployment, the 
        subset caps in `config.yaml` must be removed, and ResNet++ must be trained 
        on the full dataset (30,000+ samples) for 20-50 epochs using GPU acceleration.
